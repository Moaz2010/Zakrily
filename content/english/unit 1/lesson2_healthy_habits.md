## 4. Grammar / Language Rules

**Rule: The Present Simple Tense (زمن المضارع البسيط)**

**Usage:** We use the present simple tense to talk about daily habits and routines. (نستخدم زمن المضارع البسيط للتحدث عن العادات اليومية والروتين.)

**Formation — Affirmative Statements:**

| Subject | Verb | Rest of the sentence |
|---|---|---|
| I / You / We / They (اسم جمع) | eat | lunch at home every day. |
| He / She / It (اسم مفرد) | eats | lunch at home every day. |

With "he, she, it," we add **-s / -es / -ies** to the base form of the verb.

**Note (rules for adding the ending):**
1. We add **-s** to most verbs with He/She/It: *He play**s** football every day.*
2. We add **-es** to verbs ending in **ss, ch, sh, o, x** with He/She/It: *My brother watch**es** TV after lunch.*
3. We add **-ies** to verbs ending in a consonant + **y** (dropping the y) with He/She/It: *My sister stud**ies** English every day.*

**Remember — Verb "to be":**

| Subject | "Be" | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I'm 10 years old. |
| He / She / It (اسم مفرد) | is | He is happy. |
| You / We / They (اسم جمع) | are | They are cute. |

**Important Note:** A common area of difficulty is remembering which ending (-s, -es, or -ies) applies based on the verb's final letter(s), and remembering that the base verb form (no ending) is used with I/You/We/They.
