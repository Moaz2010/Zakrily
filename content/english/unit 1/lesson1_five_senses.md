## 1. Lesson Content

**Topic:** Our Senses (حواسنا)

The lesson introduces the five human senses, the body part associated with each sense, and example sentences describing how each sense is used.

**The Five Senses:**
- **taste** (حاسة التذوق) — We taste with our tongue. (نتذوق بلساننا)
- **touch** (حاسة اللمس) — We touch with our hands. (نلمس بأيدينا)
- **hearing** (حاسة السمع) — We hear with our ears. (نسمع بآذاننا)
- **sight** (حاسة الإبصار) — We see with our eyes. (نرى بأعيننا)
- **smell** (حاسة الشم) — We smell with our nose. (نشم بأنفنا)

## 2. Lesson Explanation / Simplified Notes

- We have **five senses**. Each sense uses a different part of our body:
  - 👅 **Taste** → tongue
  - ✋ **Touch** → hands
  - 👂 **Hearing** → ears
  - 👁️ **Sight** → eyes
  - 👃 **Smell** → nose
- Our senses help us in two big ways:
  1. **Understand the world around us** — for example, seeing colors and shapes, hearing birds sing, smelling flowers.
  2. **Stay safe** — for example, if we smell smoke, we know there might be a fire nearby.
- In the dialogue, the teacher (Ms. Mona) and two students (Sami and Salma) talk in the school garden about what they can see, hear, and smell — and why senses matter for safety.

## 6. Lesson Summary

- There are **five senses**: taste (tongue), touch (hands), hearing (ears), sight (eyes), smell (nose).
- Senses help us **understand the world around us** and **stay safe** (e.g., smelling smoke warns us of fire).
- Key vocabulary: taste, touch, hearing, sight, smell, fire, smoke, voices, tongue, safe, amazing, understand.
- Reading skill practiced: determining the meaning of words in context (synonyms/opposites), e.g., safe ↔ dangerous; amazing = wonderful.
- Exercises in this lesson mainly test **vocabulary memorization** (which organ matches which sense), **vocabulary application in context**, **reading comprehension** (literal detail and main idea), a little **analytical/inference work** (cause-and-effect, opposites/synonyms), **punctuation mechanics**, and **guided paragraph writing**.
