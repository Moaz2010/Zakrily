**Story elements (taught explicitly before the story is read):**

- **Characters** (الشخصيات): the people and animals in a story.
- **Setting** (زمان ومكان القصة): the time and place of a story.
- **Plot** (الحبكة):
  - **Beginning** — Meet the people / know the place.
  - **Problem** — Something goes wrong.
  - **Middle** — The character tries to fix it.
  - **Ending** — The problem is solved.

**The characters of this story:** Goha (the main character); the owner of the restaurant.
**The setting of this story:** Time — in the morning; Place — outside the restaurant.

## 2. Lesson Explanation / Simplified Notes

- This lesson uses a traditional folk story about **Goha** to teach students what makes up a story: its **characters**, its **setting** (time and place), and its **plot** (beginning, problem, middle, ending).
- From the "Main points" summary table, we can tell the outline of the story even before reading it in full:
  1. Goha is hungry but has no money for food.
  2. He smells delicious soup coming from a restaurant and sits outside enjoying the smell.
  3. The restaurant owner asks Goha to pay for smelling the soup.
  4. Goha cleverly offers to "pay" using the jingling sound of his coins.
  5. The owner laughs and invites Goha inside to actually share the soup.
- The two characters are Goha and the restaurant owner. The story takes place in the morning, outside a restaurant.
