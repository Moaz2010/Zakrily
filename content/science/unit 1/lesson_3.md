## 1. Lesson Overview

Lesson 3, "Colors and Shapes of Living Organisms," teaches students how to classify (group) living organisms based on observable characteristics such as color, shape, and size. Students learn the two-step process of classification: finding similarities or differences among living organisms, and then grouping organisms with similar characteristics together. Main scientific concepts covered: classification, similarities and differences among living organisms. Key skill developed: sorting and grouping living organisms according to shared physical characteristics.

---

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of Lesson 3, students should be able to:

* Recognize the concept of classification.
* Classify living organisms by color, shape, and size.
* Identify similar characteristics (such as having legs, having wings, or similar colors) among living organisms.
* Identify different characteristics (such as different shapes or different sizes) among living organisms.

## 3. Lesson Content

**Unit:** Chapter 1 | **Lesson:** Lesson 3 | **Topic:** Classifying Living Organisms by Color, Shape, and Size

### Concepts

Living organisms can be classified by observing their characteristics, such as color, shape, and size. To classify living organisms into groups, two steps must be followed: Find (similar or different characteristics), then Classify (group organisms with similar characteristics together).

### Definitions

**Definition — Classification**
The process of collecting living organisms with similar characteristics and placing them in groups.

### Scientific Facts

* Living organisms can be grouped by color, shape, or size.
* Similar characteristics used for classification include: having legs, having wings, and similar colors.
* Different characteristics used for classification include: different shapes and different sizes.

### Process — How to Group (Classify) Living Organisms

**Step 1 — Find**
Look for either:
* **Similar characteristics** — for example: has legs (cat, camel), has wings (birds), similar colors (cactus and another green plant).
* **Different characteristics** — for example: different shapes (a date palm vs. a small potted plant), different sizes (an elephant vs. an ant).

**Step 2 — Classify**
Collect living organisms with similar characteristics and place them in the same group.

### Comparisons

* **Spider vs. Sparrow (size):** The spider is smaller than the sparrow.
* **Rabbit vs. Sparrow (size):** The rabbit is larger than the sparrow.
* **Lizard vs. Spider (number of legs):** The spider has 8 legs, while the lizard has 4 legs.
* **Living organisms with the same number of legs:** For example, the butterfly and the grasshopper both have 6 legs.

### Important Notes

* Classification can be done in different ways depending on which characteristic you choose to look at (color, shape, or size) — the same set of living organisms can be grouped differently depending on the characteristic chosen.
* Living organisms with the same number of legs (or other shared traits) are placed in the same classification group.

## 5. Simplified Student Explanation

Scientists group living organisms together based on things they have in common. This is called **classification**.

To classify living organisms, you follow two steps:

1. **Find** — look for how living organisms are similar (like having legs, having wings, or the same color) or different (like different shapes or different sizes).
2. **Classify** — put the living organisms that share the same characteristic together in one group.

For example:
* You could group animals by **color**: brown animals in one group, green plants in another.
* You could group animals by **shape** (how many legs they have): a spider has 8 legs, a butterfly has 6 legs, a lizard has 4 legs, an eagle has 2 legs, and a fish or snake has no legs.
* You could group living organisms by **size**: bigger ones in one group, smaller ones in another.

The same group of living organisms can be sorted in different ways — it depends on which characteristic (color, shape, or size) you decide to look at!

## 7. Worked Examples / Scientific Reasoning

### Example 1

**Problem:**
A student has pictures of a fish, a lizard, an eagle, a butterfly, a grasshopper, and a spider. The student wants to classify them by shape (number of legs). How should the student group them?

**Solution:**
Step 1: Find — count the legs of each living organism. Fish has no legs (0), lizard has 4 legs, eagle has 2 legs, butterfly has 6 legs, grasshopper has 6 legs, spider has 8 legs.
Step 2: Classify — group organisms with the same number of legs together.

**Final Answer:**
No legs: fish. 2 legs: eagle. 4 legs: lizard. 6 legs: butterfly and grasshopper. 8 legs: spider.

## 8. Lesson Summary

### Key Ideas

* Classification is the process of collecting living organisms with similar characteristics and placing them in groups.
* Living organisms can be classified by color, shape, and size.
* To classify, first find similarities or differences, then group organisms with similar characteristics together.

### Key Facts

* Similar characteristics for grouping include: having legs, having wings, similar colors.
* Different characteristics for grouping include: different shapes, different sizes.
* The spider has 8 legs; the lizard has 4 legs; the eagle has 2 legs; the fish and snake have no legs; the butterfly and grasshopper each have 6 legs.

### Key Vocabulary

* Classification — the process of collecting living organisms with similar characteristics and placing them in groups.

### Important Relationships

* **Cause:** Two living organisms share a characteristic (e.g., both have wings). **Effect:** They can be classified in the same group.
* **Cause:** Two living organisms have different characteristics (e.g., different number of legs). **Effect:** They are classified into different groups.

### What Students Should Remember

* Classification = finding similarities/differences, then grouping.
* Living organisms can be grouped by color, shape, or size — and the same organisms can be grouped differently depending on which characteristic is chosen.
